SnapTune - installation (macOS 11 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel)

1. Copy the plugins into your plugin folders
   (in Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., then paste the path):

     SnapTune.vst3       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
     SnapTune.component  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/

   SnapTune.app is a standalone version (use it with a microphone and
   headphones); put it anywhere, e.g. Applications.

2. These builds are not notarized by Apple, so macOS may block them the
   first time. Open Terminal and run:

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/SnapTune.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/SnapTune.component

   For the standalone app, right-click SnapTune.app > Open the first time.

3. Quit your DAW completely, open it again and rescan plugins
   (FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins).
