Usage data

Once per DAW session, SnapTune sends an anonymous note to getsnaptune.com: the plugin version, the
Windows version, the CPU type, the DAW's name, the plugin format, and a random id made on this
computer. Nothing about your audio, your projects or your settings is ever sent. The server keeps
the country, never the IP address. When its window opens, SnapTune also checks once a day whether a
newer version is out.

On the next page you can untick "Send anonymous usage data" to turn both off. To change your mind
later, delete or create an empty file named no-telemetry in %APPDATA%\SnapTune.

The full privacy policy: https://getsnaptune.com/privacy
